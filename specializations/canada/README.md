# Canada Specialization

Completion token: `AGENTLOGISTICS_AL_16_CANADA_COMPLIANCE_READY`

It is isolated from universal core logistics skills because Canadian requirements can differ by federal, provincial, territorial, municipal, modal, carrier, product, hazard, customs, industry, and employer-program scope.

## Boundary

This specialization may produce research briefs, preparation checklists, evidence requests, and qualified-review handoffs. It must not provide legal advice, compliance declarations, safety approvals, certifications, customs approvals, dangerous-goods classification approvals, or live system changes.
