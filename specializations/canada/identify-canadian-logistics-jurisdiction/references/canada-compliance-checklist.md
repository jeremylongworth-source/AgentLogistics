# Canada Compliance Checklist

Completion token: AGENTLOGISTICS_AL_16_CANADA_COMPLIANCE_READY

This reference supports `identify-canadian-logistics-jurisdiction`. It is a routing and review checklist, not legal advice.

## Required Boundaries

- Identify the exact province or territory, federal context, workplace, mode, product, hazard, carrier, customs, and employer-program scope before applying any Canada-specific claim.
- Treat CCOHS as national OHS guidance and jurisdiction navigation support, not as the enforcement authority for every workplace.
- Use Canada.ca, Justice Laws, Transport Canada, CBSA, and provincial or territorial regulator sources for current rule claims when they apply.
- Keep source-backed regulatory interpretation separate from operational recommendations and approvals.
- Require qualified review before any compliance, certification, release, customs, dangerous-goods, safety, engineering, employment, or enforcement decision.

## Evidence Checklist

- Jurisdiction and regulator map
- Facility, route, mode, carrier, and activity context
- Product, hazard, SDS, item master, lot, serial, expiry, quantity, UOM, and storage evidence
- SOPs, employer safety program, inspection records, training records, maintenance records, permits, and qualified-person notes
- Shipping, TDG, customs, import, export, BOL, waybill, commercial invoice, WMS, TMS, ERP, OMS, and carrier records
- Source access dates, publication dates, amendments, conflicts, and gaps

## Blocked Outputs

- legal conclusion
- compliance declaration
- safety approval
- equipment certification
- operator certification
- TDG classification approval
- customs declaration approval
- import or export release approval
- vehicle roadworthiness certification
- driver qualification approval
- fire, building, structural, environmental, or engineering approval
- live system or production record change
