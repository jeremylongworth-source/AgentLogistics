# Canadian Logistics Authority Map

Completion token: `AGENTLOGISTICS_AL_16_CANADA_COMPLIANCE_READY`

This map supports the AL-16 Canada specialization. It identifies source categories and review boundaries for Canadian logistics safety and compliance research. It does not approve compliance or replace qualified review.

## Critical Rule

Do not invent a single unified Canadian warehouse law. Requirements can differ by jurisdiction, workplace, activity, industry, mode, carrier, product, hazard, customs status, and employer program.

## Review Boundary

Canada specialization outputs are research briefs, preparation checklists, evidence requests, and qualified-review handoffs. They must not declare compliance, approve operational changes, certify equipment or operators, classify dangerous goods, file customs declarations, or issue legal advice.
