# Dangerous Goods Checklist

Completion token: `AGENTLOGISTICS_AL_21_DANGEROUS_GOODS_READY`

Use current official sources before making dangerous-goods, hazardous-materials,
transportation, storage, segregation, packaging, marking, labeling,
documentation, training, incident, environmental, customs, carrier, or
jurisdiction-specific claims.

Review checklist:

- Identify material or article, SDS or classification source, hazard class or
  division if supplied, packing group if supplied, UN number or proper shipping
  name if supplied, quantity, packaging, jurisdiction, route, mode, facility or
  carrier context, and requested decision.
- Separate classification, packaging, marking, labeling, documentation, storage,
  segregation, transport mode, jurisdiction, and personnel qualification
  evidence.
- Separate user evidence, official sources, assumptions, source conflicts,
  observations, inferences, operational next steps, and qualified-review needs.
- Label missing material identity, classification evidence, quantity, packaging,
  route, mode, carrier, storage, training, emergency response, environmental,
  customs, and source records.
- Do not state that a material, package, document, storage plan, segregation
  plan, shipment, employee, facility, vehicle, route, mode, carrier, incident
  response, cleanup, disposal, or system change is compliant, safe, legal,
  approved, certified, accepted, or ready.
- Block legal advice, compliance declarations, safety approvals,
  certifications, dangerous-goods classification approvals, hazardous materials
  classification approvals, packaging design approvals, marking or labeling
  approvals, shipping paper approvals, emergency response approvals,
  environmental determinations, carrier approvals, personnel qualification
  certifications, customs approvals, customer commitment approvals, financial
  approvals, and live system changes.
