# Food Cold-Chain Checklist

Completion token: `AGENTLOGISTICS_AL_20_FOOD_COLD_CHAIN_READY`

Use current official sources before making food, cold-chain, transportation,
traceability, recall, sanitation, preventive-control, import/export, carrier,
customer, product-release, or jurisdiction-specific claims.

Food and cold-chain requirements are product-specific and
jurisdiction-specific.

## Required Checks

- Identify product, jurisdiction, facility, mode, route, customer, carrier,
  temperature basis, lot or expiry basis, inventory status, and requested
  decision.
- Separate user evidence, official sources, assumptions, source conflicts,
  observations, inferences, operational next steps, and qualified-review needs.
- Label missing temperature, lot, expiry, custody, cleaning, equipment,
  monitoring, hold, recall, customer, carrier, and system records.
- Do not state that food, inventory, a shipment, a vehicle, a facility, a
  process, a recall, or a control program is compliant, safe, legal, approved,
  certified, saleable, or releasable.
- Block legal advice, compliance declarations, food safety approvals, product
  release approvals, temperature excursion disposition approvals, recall
  initiation approvals, sanitation approvals, equipment certifications, carrier
  approvals, customer commitment approvals, financial approvals, and live system
  changes.
