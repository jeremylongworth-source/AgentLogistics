## Source Categories

- FDA sanitary transportation sources cover shipper, loader, carrier, receiver,
  vehicle, equipment, operations, record, training, and waiver research for
  covered US food transportation contexts.
- FDA traceability sources cover Critical Tracking Event and Key Data Element
  research for foods subject to additional traceability records, with current
  compliance-date and enforcement status requiring verification before use.
- FDA recall guidance sources cover recall research, model communications, and
  industry recall preparation for FDA-regulated products.
- USDA FSIS sources cover meat, poultry, and egg-product recall and food-safety
  handling research where FSIS authority is relevant.
- CFIA preventive-control and traceability sources cover Canadian food business
  preventive controls, recall procedure, traceability, and recall simulation
  research where Canadian jurisdiction is relevant.
- Local SOPs, customer requirements, carrier agreements, product
  specifications, labels, SDSs, quality programs, food safety plans, equipment
  manuals, temperature records, and system records remain required evidence for
  operational decisions.

## Required Boundaries

- Do not state that a food, lot, facility, vehicle, trailer, route, process,
  temperature history, sanitation state, recall scope, traceability program, or
  preventive-control program is compliant, approved, certified, safe, legal,
  saleable, or releasable.
- Do not approve food safety plans, HACCP plans, preventive control plans,
  sanitation procedures, allergen controls, temperature excursion dispositions,
  shelf-life extensions, recall decisions, public notices, customer notices,
  regulator submissions, inventory release, disposal, donations, carrier
