# International Logistics Specialization

Completion token: `AGENTLOGISTICS_AL_22_INTERNATIONAL_LOGISTICS_READY`

The international-logistics specialization provides source-backed logistics
research and planning support for cross-border and multi-country shipments.
In this specialization, international logistics requirements are
lane-specific and jurisdiction-specific, and may involve qualified customs
broker, freight forwarder, legal, trade compliance, sanctions, export-control,
carrier, port, terminal, insurer, customer, or financial review.

## Packages

- `map-cross-border-logistics-flow`
- `prepare-international-shipment-document-research`
- `plan-customs-broker-handoff`
- `triage-port-terminal-exception`

## Source Rule

Use current official sources for regulatory, customs, export, sanctions,
documentation, port, and terminal claims. The source starting points in
`references/international-logistics-source-map.md` were verified on 2026-09-03
and must be refreshed before operational use.

## Boundary

This specialization may produce research briefs, preparation checklists,
evidence requests, routing briefs, customs broker packets, international
shipment document research packets, cross-border flow maps, port or terminal
exception handoffs, and qualified-review questions. It must not provide legal
advice, compliance declarations, customs entry approvals, customs release
approvals, export filing approvals, sanctions determinations, export-control
classification approvals, license requirement determinations, duty or tax
determinations, Incoterms contract advice, bill of lading approvals, commercial
invoice approvals, packing list approvals, carrier approvals, port or terminal
release approvals, freight forwarder approvals, financial approvals, customer
commitment approvals, or live system changes.
