# United States Logistics Authority Map

Completion token: `AGENTLOGISTICS_AL_17_US_COMPLIANCE_READY`

This map supports the AL-17 United States specialization. It identifies source categories and review boundaries for US logistics safety and compliance research. It does not approve compliance or replace qualified review.

## Critical Rule

Do not invent a single unified US warehouse law. Requirements can differ by federal, state, territorial, local, workplace, activity, industry, mode, carrier, product, hazard, customs status, environmental status, and employer program.
