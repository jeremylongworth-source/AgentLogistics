# US Compliance Checklist

Completion token: AGENTLOGISTICS_AL_17_US_COMPLIANCE_READY

This reference supports `research-us-storage-requirements`. It is a routing and review checklist, not legal advice.

## Required Boundaries

- Identify the exact state or territory, federal context, OSHA state-plan status, workplace, mode, product, hazard, carrier, customs, environmental, and employer-program scope before applying any US-specific claim.
- Treat OSHA, DOT, PHMSA, FMCSA, CBP, EPA, eCFR, and state or territorial sources according to their actual authority and scope.
- Use current official sources for current rule claims when they apply.
- Keep source-backed regulatory interpretation separate from operational recommendations and approvals.
- Require qualified review before any compliance, certification, release, customs, hazardous-materials, safety, engineering, environmental, employment, or enforcement decision.

## Evidence Checklist

- Jurisdiction and regulator map
- Facility, route, mode, carrier, and activity context
- Product, hazard, SDS, item master, lot, serial, expiry, quantity, UOM, and storage evidence
- SOPs, employer safety program, inspection records, training records, maintenance records, permits, and qualified-person notes
- Shipping, hazmat, customs, import, export, BOL, waybill, commercial invoice, WMS, TMS, ERP, OMS, and carrier records
- Source access dates, publication dates, amendments, conflicts, and gaps
