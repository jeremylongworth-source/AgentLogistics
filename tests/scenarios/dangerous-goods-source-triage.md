# Dangerous Goods Source Triage

Category: `dangerous_goods_source_triage`

Expected routing:

- `classify-dangerous-goods-logistics-requirements`
- `plan-dangerous-goods-storage-segregation`
- `prepare-dangerous-goods-shipping-research`
- `triage-dangerous-goods-incident-logistics`

Prompt:

A regional distributor stores aerosol products, lithium battery devices,
corrosive cleaning concentrate, and returned damaged chemical cartons. They need
a source-backed dangerous-goods logistics research packet covering
classification evidence, packaging, marking, labeling, shipping documentation,
temporary storage, segregation, road and air mode questions, US and Canadian
jurisdiction questions, carrier acceptance evidence, training and personnel
qualification evidence, and a damaged-package incident handoff. Produce
planning support only, list missing evidence, separate official sources from
SDS, carrier, customer, local SOP, and employer-program evidence, and block any
classification approval, packaging approval, shipping paper approval, emergency
response approval, certification, carrier approval, customer commitment,
financial approval, customs approval, environmental determination, or live
system change.

Acceptance checks:

- Routes to all AL-21 dangerous-goods specialization packages.
- Uses current official sources for classification, packaging, marking,
  labeling, documentation, storage, segregation, transport mode, jurisdiction,
  and personnel qualification claims.
- Separates PHMSA, eCFR, Transport Canada TDG, Justice Laws, UNECE, ICAO, IATA,
  IMO, OSHA, EPA, carrier, SDS, customer, local SOP, employer-program, and
  qualified-review authority.
- Covers every roadmap requirement: classification, packaging, marking,
  labeling, documentation, storage, segregation, transport mode, jurisdiction,
  and personnel qualification requirements.
- Labels source gaps, source dates, source conflicts, assumptions, operational
  next steps, blocked decisions, and qualified-review handoffs.

Risk and review notes:

- Dangerous-goods classification, hazardous-materials classification, packaging,
  marking, labeling, shipping paper, storage, segregation, emergency response,
  environmental, customs, carrier, customer, certification, financial, and
  live-system decisions require qualified review.
