Prompt:

A regional distributor handles refrigerated meals, frozen ingredients, and
short-dated prepared food across two warehouses and multiple carriers. They need
a source-backed food and cold-chain logistics research packet covering
temperature-controlled storage, monitoring records, a suspected temperature
excursion, FEFO rotation, expiry-controlled inventory, lot traceability,
sanitation-sensitive handling, food segregation, recall logistics, cold-chain
transportation, and handoff requirements. Produce planning support only, list
missing evidence, separate US and Canadian official-source questions, and block
any product release, food safety approval, recall decision, certification,
financial approval, customer commitment, carrier approval, or live system
change.

Acceptance checks:

- Routes to all AL-20 food-cold-chain specialization packages.
- Uses current official sources for regulatory, traceability, sanitary
  transportation, recall, and preventive-control claims.
- Separates FDA, USDA FSIS, CFIA, customer, carrier, local SOP, product
  specification, equipment, and qualified-review authority.
- Covers every roadmap capability: temperature-controlled storage, temperature
  monitoring, excursion handling, FEFO, expiry controls, lot traceability,
  sanitation-sensitive logistics, food segregation, recall logistics,
  cold-chain transportation, and cold-chain handoffs.
- Labels source gaps, source dates, source conflicts, assumptions, operational
  next steps, blocked decisions, and qualified-review handoffs.

Risk and review notes:

- Food safety, temperature excursion disposition, recall scope, sanitation,
  compliance, certification, customer commitment, carrier approval, financial,
  and live-system decisions require qualified review.
- AgentLogistics and ChefSkills remain independent with no hard cross-project
  dependency.
